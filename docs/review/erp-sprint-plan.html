How each sprint runs. One branch from main per sprint, a pull request per workstream where it helps review (sprint 2's reservation table goes in on its own). Every server change lands with a test in server/test/; every screen change lands with a screenshot under docs/review/screens/. CI must be green before merge. At the end of sprints 1 to 3 the day simulation is re-run and compared with the sprint 0 baseline, and the master list is regenerated with the closed items.

Sprint 1

Close the integrity holes and take the quick wins

1½–2 weeks

Every item here is a small change with a test. Together they remove the last known ways to lose stock or money, take the two daily irritations that cost the most time (queue order, the end-of-day report), and fix the three things the invoicing walkthrough turned up. Nothing here changes the data model except one flag and one foreign-key change on customers.

What changes for the business
  • A return can no longer be credited twice; a customer with history cannot be deleted, only made inactive.
  • Undo Last Shipment refuses when the units have already come back on a return.
  • Validation and Allocation work oldest-first with a Skip button; Logistics gets a shipped-today report with partial shipments.
  • Receiving and the Back Order Queue stop downloading every PO ever written; a report with an impossible item filter no longer downloads the year.
Done means
  • Every row below has a passing test in server/test/ or a Playwright check where the change is only on screen.
  • CI green; sim/day.mjs re-run with no regression against the sprint 0 baseline.
  • Master list updated: A-13, A-14, A-15, A-16, A-17, A-18, A-20, A-21, B-07, B-18, C-03, C-05, C-08 (first half), C-09, C-10, C-11, C-15, D-05, D-06, D-07, D-09, E-01 closed; B-06, D-02 partial.
Decisions needed first
  • B-06: when do prices lock? Proposed: from Allocated onward only Order Entry or Sales Order View edit may change a rate; from Released onward nobody.
  • A-15: once an invoice has reached QuickBooks, refuse Undo Last Shipment outright (proposed), or allow it with a reason?
  • B-07: Import creates orders only, never edits (proposed yes).
  • C-05: must every order have a linked customer? Walk-in orders still invoice, so proposed: required in Order Entry, optional on import.

Sprint 2

Reservation table, read exposure, audit in the transaction

2½–3 weeks · delivered 29 Sep 2026

One structural change, the reservation table, and the medium items that either depend on it or were waiting for a quieter sprint. After this the server holds every number that matters (on hand, reserved, available) and the browser stops recomputing any of them. Delivered 29 Sep 2026 on branch claude/sweet-mccarthy-iug4f3 (eight batches, 79 server tests and 5 browser specs green); decisions taken: A-07 release the remainder to the queue, B-10 prices for Order Entry, Sales Order View, Customer Pricing and Invoices, C-17 renamed on screen only. Open leftovers: D-12 main bundle 524 KB, E-02 PO cost default. Also built on request: the invoice review queue with charges, Preferences with a dark theme, notes on printed pick lists, the server-computed estimated ship date.

What changes for the business
  • Availability is a column, not a scan: the allocation pages, Inventory and the capacity banner open in under a second at a year of data.
  • A partly released order releases the reservation on what it did not ship (A-07), and the on-PO figure is exact (A-06).
  • A Checked order that is changed goes back for checking; cancelling a printed order leaves a pull-from-floor task instead of vanishing.
  • Prices are visible only to roles that need them; every price-sheet change is logged row by row.
  • A browser test walks the five core flows on every push.
Done means
  • Invariant in the workload test: for every item, the sum of open Allocation.qty equals Item.qtyReserved, and reserved never exceeds on hand.
  • No page under src/ computes availability or reservations from order JSON (grep for allocatedQty outside the order stores returns nothing).
  • Playwright smoke green in CI; master list updated: A-06, A-07, A-22, A-23, A-25, B-08, B-10, B-11, C-01, C-06, C-07, C-12–C-17, D-02, D-08, D-10–D-13, E-03, F-01 closed.
Decisions needed first
  • A-07: when a partial shipment leaves a line back-ordered, keep its reservation (holds stock for the customer) or release it (frees stock for the queue)? Proposed: keep, and show it on the Back Order Queue.
  • B-10: which roles see prices? Proposed: Order Entry, Sales Order View, Customer Service, Sales Manager, Director, Admin; warehouse and receiving roles see none.
  • C-17: rename the status Pick & Packed to Released.

Sprint 3

Visibility on the floor, a human at every check, back orders flagged on receipt

3–4 weeks · scoped 1 Oct 2026

Revised on 1 October after sprint 2: no order is ever checked or allocated by the system, and a back order is flagged for an analyst when its stock arrives rather than filled. The sprint adds the stage the floor was missing (Ready to ship, with the packing slip printed from what was actually packed), a dock screen for the warehouse, the order claim that ends queue collisions, release and print in one step, the BOL and label built from the order with the carrier details saved for Customer Service, receive-all, item cost and margin, and the Order Entry grid.

What changes for the business
  • Every order is checked by a person other than the one who entered it; nothing is allocated without an analyst.
  • The floor has its own screen: Being picked, Ready to ship, Shipped at pickup, Pull from floor. The packing slip is printed from what was packed, so a short never needs a reprint.
  • A back order whose stock just arrived is flagged on the queue and the Dashboard the moment the receipt posts; an analyst decides what it gets.
  • Two analysts never open the same order. Release prints the pick list in the same click.
  • Customer Service can answer "which truck, which PRO" from Sales Order View.
  • Order Entry takes two touches per line instead of four; Receiving fills a whole PO in one click.
Done means
  • Server tests for every rule here (maker not checker, ready-to-ship quantities, the flag on receipt, the claim lease, cost on POs); the workload test runs with the new stage in the mix.
  • Playwright: the floor flow (print, pack check with a short, ready, shipped at pickup) and a five-line order entered by keyboard only.
  • Master list updated: C-08, G-05, G-06 (as a flag), G-07, G-08, G-09, G-10 (maker-not-checker), G-11, E-02, E-05 closed; G-03 and G-04 withdrawn by decision.
Decisions needed first
  • Taken 1 Oct: back orders are flagged on any receipt of their item, with full / partial / none coverage shown (not filled); the checker must not be the writer, enforced on the server (Admin exempt); the pack check is done by a warehouse login on a dock screen, which prints the packing slip and sets Ready to ship; short picks are recorded at the pack check and Mark Shipped ships exactly what was packed; carrier, SCAC, PRO and pickup date are saved on the order; G-11, G-08, E-05/E-02 and G-09 are in.
  • Also taken 1 Oct: the dock screen uses one shared floor login (the Warehouse preset); the pack check is stamped as the floor, with the packer's initials typed on the pack check so the record still says who. Logistics may also mark an order Ready to ship from Open Picks, same command, same stamp.
